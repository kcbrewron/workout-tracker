# Workout Tracker - Product Requirements Document

## Overview
A personalized workout tracking application that helps individuals monitor their fitness progress, plan workouts, and achieve their fitness goals through intelligent recommendations and comprehensive tracking.

## Core Functionality

### User Identification & Onboarding
- **User Profile Creation**: Secure user registration and authentication
- **Goal Setting**: Users define their fitness objectives (weight loss, muscle gain, endurance, strength, sport-specific training)
- **Experience Assessment**: Evaluate current fitness level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- **Sport-Specific Preferences**: Allow users to specify sport-focused training needs (running, cycling, weightlifting, CrossFit, etc.)

### Initial User Assessment
1. **Fitness Goals Questionnaire**:
   - Primary objective (strength, endurance, weight loss, muscle gain, athletic performance)
   - Target timeline and milestones
   - Available workout frequency and duration

2. **Experience Level Evaluation**:
   - Current exercise routine and frequency
   - Familiarity with different exercise types
   - Previous training experience or certifications

3. **Sport-Specific Requests**:
   - Preferred activities or sports
   - Competition goals or recreational focus
   - Equipment availability and gym access

### Core Workout Tracking Features
- **Exercise Library**: Comprehensive database of exercises with instructions and form videos
- **Workout Planning**: Create custom workout routines or use AI-suggested plans
- **Progress Tracking**: Record sets, reps, weights, duration, and personal records
- **Performance Analytics**: Visual progress charts and trend analysis
- **Adaptive Programming**: AI-driven workout adjustments based on performance data

### Personalization Engine
- **Smart Recommendations**: Suggest workouts based on goals, experience, and progress
- **Recovery Tracking**: Monitor rest days and workout intensity
- **Progression Logic**: Automatically adjust difficulty and volume over time
- **Injury Prevention**: Identify potential overtraining and suggest modifications

## Success Metrics
- User engagement and retention rates
- Goal achievement tracking
- Progressive overload adherence
- User satisfaction scores

## Technical Considerations
- Mobile-responsive design for gym use
- Offline capability for workout logging
- Data export functionality
- Integration with fitness wearables (future enhancement)
